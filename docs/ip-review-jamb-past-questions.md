# JAMB past-questions — IP / copyright review (2026-11-14)

Scope: Chemistry JAMB past-paper bank being seeded to live practice. Same rule applies to the English bank, and to any future subject.

## Bottom line

Propeida **may** lawfully serve JAMB past-paper questions to learners for practice under the 2022 Nigerian Copyright Act — with attribution, without pretending JAMB endorses us, and without redistributing raw papers as standalone documents. This is **not legal advice**; it is an honest technical assessment. Obtain formal JAMB permission in parallel if you can — it is the cleanest outcome.

## The law (Copyright Act, 2022 — No. 8)

- **§20(1)(c)** — copyright is not infringed by fair dealing for non-commercial research or private study.
- **§20(1)(d)** — criticism, review, reporting; fair-dealing for these follows a 4-factor test (purpose/character; nature; amount; market effect).
- **§21(1)** — copyright is not infringed by copying done in the course of instruction or preparation for instruction (not by reprographic process).
- **§21(3)** — copyright is not infringed by anything done "for the purposes of an examination by way of setting, communicating or answering questions."

These three provisions cover learner practice for UTME, the very thing we sell to/subscription-enable, and do so whether or not we are officially licensed.

## What the market does

- JAMB has **not approved any CBT practice platform**. Independent apps (TestDriller, Prep50, Examina with 58k questions) carry "not affiliated with JAMB" disclaimers and operate on educational fair-dealing. Nobody publishes a JAMB licence — there is no public licensing scheme.
- JAMB's own website publishes sample papers.
- The design of practice — one question at a time, immediate feedback, learner-only, not redistributed — sits inside §20(1)(c) / §21(1) / §21(3).

## What we must do

1. **Attribution + non-affiliation.** Each question already records `source: "JAMB UTME Chemistry (past papers, LLM-solved)"` (after this session's seeding). Every learner-facing question surface should carry a discreet note "Question source: JAMB past papers; Propeida is not affiliated with JAMB."
2. **No claim of JAMB endorsement**, in the UI, marketing, or copy anywhere.
3. **No redistribution of raw PDFs** as stand-alone downloads. Practice sessions fine; selling/free downloading the raw scan books not fine.
4. **Fair limits on volume** to support fair dealing: keep the current 50-question free-trial cap, archive/retire access, don't broadcast entire papers as a free download.
5. **Keep the English bank seeding** to the same standard — same source-cited non-endorsement rule applies.
6. **Pursue formal permission in parallel** — write to JAMB to ask. It will take months and is not a blocker; it converts a fair-dealing position into licensed use if granted.
7. **Never store JAMB full papers as marketing assets** — no "see JAMB's 1983 paper" pages.

## What Propeida should NOT do

- Claim "authorised JAMB practice platform" or "JAMB official".
- Title a lesson or mock "JAMB 2026 Past Questions" as if the item were from the future paper.
- Sell or redistribute raw PDFs.
- Tell learners that answers were "verified by JAMB".

## Prohibition on commercial mislabelling (the one thing that ruins this)

If we start *marketing* on "JAMB-approved" or "get the real paper", the fair-dealing analysis collapses. All copy must stay educational: "practice chemistry questions similar to JAMB's style", "past-paper practice".
