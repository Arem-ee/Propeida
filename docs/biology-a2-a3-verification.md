# Biology A.2 and A.3 verification

Date: 2 November 2026
Scope: the eight A.2 lessons and five A.3 lessons in
`content-folder/biology-curriculum.json`. A.1 was verified in the previous
Biology commit and is not re-audited here.

The purpose of this file is to show what was checked, against what, and what
changed as a result. It is a working audit, not a claim that every sentence has
an external citation.

## Sources

- **Official syllabus:** `content-folder/jamb-syllabus/structured/biology.json`,
  extracted from the JAMB/IBASS Biology syllabus at
  <https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Biology.0075e70afd357d7a39ae.pdf>.
  Every A.2/A.3 item and objective below is taken from that file.
- **External checks:** used for claims that are easy to state loosely. The
  register in section 5 lists the source for each one.
- **Standard textbook facts:** basic classification, organ and life-cycle
  material that is not individually fetched is marked "syllabus/textbook" in
  the register. It still has to agree with the syllabus wording and the lesson's
  own checks.

| Adaptive Colouration | Cryptic colouration | Countershading | Mimicry |

One check was tightened during the audit. *Plant Divisions* previously asked
which innovation "first allowed plants to reproduce without water" and keyed
"seeds and pollen". It now asks which innovation removed the need for water for
**fertilisation** and keys **pollen**, because pollen is the structure that
carries the male gamete; the seed protects the embryo rather than delivering the
gamete.

## 7. Corrections made during this audit

1. Added moss, fern and seed-plant life-cycle stages to *Plant Divisions*.
2. Added the *Taenia* two-host life cycle to *Invertebrate Phyla*.
3. Added termite and bee development to *Social Insects*, and fish, frog,
   reptile, bird and mammal stages to *Vertebrate Classes*.
4. Added the economic importance of vertebrates to *Vertebrate Classes*.
5. Added the resource function of territory to *Defence and Mating
   Adaptations*.
6. Made skin, hair and feathers explicit in *Temperature and Water Regulation*,
   and separated sweating (skin) from panting (mouth and lungs).
7. Replaced the camel-hump "metabolic water" claim with fat as an energy reserve
   plus kidney and nose water conservation.
8. Added pangolin scales as keratin and tied both reptile and mammalian scales
   to water loss and protection.
9. Changed "fungi and plants build bodies from many cells" to "most fungi and
   plants", because yeasts are unicellular.
10. Changed the biennial "edible part is the storage organ" to "first-year
    vegetative growth", which also fits cabbage.
11. Tightened the plant-reproduction check from "seeds and pollen" to "pollen",
    and changed the takeaway to "Seeds and pollen freed plants from standing
    water."

## 8. Remaining limits

- The life histories are summary traces, not full labelled diagrams. If the
  question bank starts asking for named larval stages (for example, the
  oncosphere or cysticercus by name), add those to the relevant lesson or the
  depth tier.
- Warning colouration in mushrooms is required by the syllabus and is taught
  with the fly agaric. The lesson deliberately says a bright cap "can be"
  warning colouration because the aposematic function is debated; it does not
  claim that every bright mushroom is poisonous.
- Some examples are global standard examples (poison-dart frog, peacock)
  because they are the clearest illustrations. Nigerian examples are used where
  the syllabus supplies them: maize, water leaf, mango, cashew, oil palm,
  tilapia, catfish and *Agama*.
- These lessons have no optional depth tier yet. Depth remains quality debt
  across the library, not a correctness issue.
