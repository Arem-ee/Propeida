# JAMB Economics 2014 batch — owner review sheet

16 audited questions from the 2014 paper. Six imported keys were wrong and corrected (demand shift, supply-function quantity, fixed costs, stagflation, money market, quota). The rest of the paper was fused, figure-dependent or ambiguous and was rejected.

**A change in demand for a normal good implies that there is ______.**

- A. a shift in the demand curve
- B. a change in the quantity demanded as price changes
- C. a movement along a given demand curve
- D. a change in the price elasticity of demand

**Key: A** — A change in demand means the whole relationship has changed, so the curve shifts. A change in the good’s own price causes a movement along the existing curve.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q9 (key corrected)*

**Given the supply function P = ¼(Qs + 10), when P = N10, Qs is ______.**

- A. 20
- B. 30
- C. 15
- D. 50

**Key: B** — Substitute P = 10: 10 = ¼(Qs + 10). Multiply both sides by 4 to get 40 = Qs + 10, so Qs = 30.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q13 (key corrected)*

**The price mechanism determines the prices of commodities through ______.**

- A. auctioning
- B. the sale of treasury bills
- C. market forces
- D. government legislation

**Key: C** — In a market economy, the interaction of demand and supply — market forces — sets price. Treasury bills and legislation are not price-setting mechanisms for goods.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q16*

**Rent and administrative expenses are examples of ______.**

- A. average fixed costs
- B. average variable costs
- C. variable costs
- D. fixed costs

**Key: D** — Rent and administrative salaries are paid even when output is zero, so they are fixed costs. Average fixed cost is a per-unit figure, not the cost itself.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q21 (key corrected)*

**If the marginal propensity to consume is 2/3 and investment is N100 million, the level of national income is ______.**

- A. N300 million
- B. N30 million
- C. N40 million
- D. N150 million

**Key: A** — The multiplier is 1 ÷ (1 − MPC) = 1 ÷ (1/3) = 3. National income = 3 × N100 million = N300 million.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q26 (options rebuilt)*

**Inflation that co-exists with a high rate of unemployment is called ______.**

- A. hyperinflation
- B. stagflation
- C. demand-pull inflation
- D. cost-push inflation

**Key: B** — Stagflation combines rising prices with stagnant output and high unemployment. Demand-pull and cost-push inflation describe causes, not the unemployment combination.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q28 (key corrected)*

**Short-term loans for investment are usually obtained through the ______.**

- A. stock market
- B. development banks
- C. money market
- D. capital market

**Key: C** — The money market handles short-term funds such as treasury bills and commercial paper. The capital market and development banks supply medium- and long-term finance.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q30 (key corrected)*

**A wage freeze is a policy measure aimed at ______.**

- A. encouraging investors
- B. regulating the standard of living
- C. curbing deflation
- D. curbing inflation

**Key: D** — A wage freeze holds down labour costs and disposable income, which slows cost-push and demand-pull pressure on prices. It is an anti-inflation measure.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q32*

**A major feature of an underdeveloped economy is ______.**

- A. a low level of standard of living
- B. excess capacity utilisation
- C. a low rate of population growth
- D. a low level of unemployment

**Key: A** — Underdeveloped economies are characterised by low income per head and therefore a low standard of living, alongside low capital formation and high underemployment.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q34*

**An important role of agriculture in an economy is ______.**

- A. processing of raw materials
- B. provision of employment
- C. regulation of the price system
- D. provision of infrastructure

**Key: B** — Agriculture employs a large share of the labour force and supplies food and raw materials. Processing raw materials is manufacturing, not farming.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q35*

**An advantage of large-scale farming over peasant farming is ______.**

- A. redistributing national income across regions
- B. encouraging the use of traditional implements
- C. conducting research and employing labour on a large scale
- D. encouraging urban-rural migration

**Key: C** — Large-scale farms can afford research, machinery and specialised labour, which raise yields. Traditional implements and income redistribution are not advantages of scale.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q36 (wording repaired)*

**One major factor that determines the location of an industry is ______.**

- A. a tax exemption grant
- B. the capital base of the firm
- C. the social responsibility of the firm
- D. its proximity to the market

**Key: D** — Firms locate near markets to cut transport costs and reach customers, especially when the product is bulky or perishable. Capital base affects scale, not location.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q38*

**The major contribution of OPEC to the Nigerian economy is the provision of ______.**

- A. stabilisation of oil prices
- B. social infrastructure
- C. subsidies on petroleum products
- D. refineries

**Key: A** — OPEC coordinates production among oil-exporting countries to stabilise prices. It does not build infrastructure, pay subsidies or refine crude oil.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q39*

**The short-run average variable cost of a firm will rise owing to ______.**

- A. the expansion of factory space
- B. an increase in the cost of labour
- C. the building of a new warehouse
- D. an increase in the salaries of directors

**Key: B** — Variable costs change with output, and labour is a variable input. Factory space, warehouses and directors’ salaries are fixed costs in the short run.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q41*

**Firms embark on vertical integration in order to ______.**

- A. take over markets formerly controlled by other firms
- B. prevent other firms from entering the market
- C. enjoy economies of large-scale production
- D. reduce advertisement and management costs

**Key: C** — Vertical integration combines stages of production or distribution, which can lower costs and secure supply. Taking over a competitor’s market is horizontal integration.

*Source: JAMB 2014 Q42*

**A trade control measure that limits imports to a definite quantity is called a ______.**

- A. excise duty
- B. import duty
- C. tariff
- D. quota

**Key: D** — A quota sets a physical limit on the quantity imported. A tariff is a tax on imports, and an excise duty is a tax on domestic production.
