## General objectives

- Art
- ART
- GENERAL OBJECTIVES
- The aim of the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) syllabus in Art is to prepare the
- candidates for the Board’s examination. It is designed to test their achievement of the course objectives,
- which are to:
- 1. exhibit knowledge of the fundamental elements, principles and terminologies of art;
- 2. show knowledge of historical dimensions of art with emphasis on Nigerian arts and crafts;
- 3. demonstrate knowledge of artistic techniques, processes, the use of materials and maintenance
- of tools and equipment;
- 4. display aesthetic awareness of the environment, meaning and functions of art in society.
- DETAILED SYLLABUS

## 

### 1. CLASSIFICATION OF ART

**Topics and notes**

- a. Visual Arts: (Fine and Applied Arts)
- b. Performing Arts: (Music, Dance and Drama)
- c. Literary Arts: (Poetry, Prose, Recital) etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- i. differentiate between the three branches of art;

### 2. ELEMENTS AND PRINCIPLES OF

**Topics and notes**

- DESIGN:
- a. Elements: line, colour, shape, form, texture, tone, value, space etc.
- b. Principles: balance, rhythm, proportion, harmony, contrast, repetition, dominance, variety, etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- ii. identify the elements of design with the aid of illustration;
- iii. analyze the principles of design with the aid of illustration;

### 3. ART TERMS

**Topics and notes**

- Pigments, motif, greenware, armature, silhouette, chiaroscuro, cire-perdue, terra- cotta etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- iv. identify art terms;
- v. link the terms to their areas of art;
- vi. use the terms in analyzing artworks.

## 

### 1. HISTORICAL DIMENSIONS OF ART

**Topics and notes**

- a. Prehistoric, Greek and Roman Art; Medieval Art: architecture, surface decorations and calligraphy;
- b. Renaissance Art and Artists: Giotto Di Bondone, Michelangelo Buonarroti, Leonardo da Vinci and Raphael (Raffaello Santi)etc.
- c. 19th and 20th Century Art Movements; Impressionism, Realism, Futurism, Cubism, Bauhaus, Pop art, Abstract v. Expressionism and Fauvism.

**Candidates should be able to**

- i. compare their materials, styles and techniques;
- ii. classify the periods and styles with emphasis on architecture, surface decoration and calligraphy;
- iii. compare the artists, their works and styles;
- iv. differentiate between the various art movements;
- v. analyze their styles techniques, innovations and influences;

### 2. TRADITIONAL AFRICAN ART

**Topics and notes**

- Egypt, Ashanti, Dogon, Mossi, Fon, Senufo, Bambara, Mende, Kissi, Bamileke and Bakumba.

**Candidates should be able to**

- vi. categorize works in terms of style, materials and locations;

### 3. TRADITIONAL NIGERIAN ART

**Topics and notes**

- Nok, Igbo-Ukwu, Ife, Benin, Esie, Igala, Jukun, Akwanshi, and Mbari.

**Candidates should be able to**

- vii. analyze the works in terms of functions, characteristics and locations;

### 4. NIGERIAN CRAFTS

**Topics and notes**

- Pottery, woodworks, cloth-weaving, carving, leather works, metal works, beadworks, body decoration, mat and cane weaving.

**Candidates should be able to**

- viii. trace the origins, locations and styles;

### 5. DEVELOPMENT IN CONTEMPORARY

**Topics and notes**

- NIGERIAN ARTS AND ARTISTS:
- a. Art Schools: Zaria, Nsukka, Osogbo group etc.
- b. Nigerian artists and art educators; Aina Onabolu, Ben Enwonwu, S. I. Wangboje, Jimoh Akolo, Dele Jegede etc.
- c. Museums, galleries and art centres;
- d. Art institutions, cultural art centres, national art organizations – Nigerian Society for Education through Arts (NSEA), Society for Nigerian Artists (S.N.A), National Council for Arts and Culture (N.C.A.C), etc.
- e. Major Festivals: Argungu Fishing Festival, Eyo, Egungun, Iri-ji (New Yam), Durbar, Igue, Ekpo, Odo, etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- ix. determine the influence of the art schools and groups towards the development of art;
- x. assess the artists in terms of their works, specializations, techniques and styles;
- xi. analyze the functions of museums, galleries, art centres;
- xii. evaluate their impact on the development of art; assess their roles and functions;
- xiii. examine the characteristics of major festivals of art and culture.

## 

### 1. ARTISTIC SKILLS, TECHNIQUES AND PROCESSES

**Topics and notes**

- a. Two-Dimensional Art: drawing, painting, graphics and textile design.
- b. Perspective:
- i. Linear, angular, aerial, parallel etc.
- ii. Perspective terms: foreground, picture plane, eye-level, vanishing point, foreshortening, optical illusion, depth etc.
- c. Sculpture, Ceramics and Crafts,
- d. Computer Graphics: CorelDraw

**Candidates should be able to**

- Candidates should be conversant with:
- i. the techniques, skills and processes with emphasis on tone, composition and colour application;
- ii. the types of perspective;
- iii. the use of perspective rules and terms;
- iv. techniques, skills and processes;
- v. the basic tools of designs

### 2. TOOLS, MATERIALS AND

**Topics and notes**

- EQUIPMENT
- a. Two-Dimensional Art Tools: brushes, lino cutters, drawing instruments, calligraphic pens, sharpeners etc.
- b. Two-dimensional Art Materials: pencils charcoal, pastel, crayon, fixative, fabric, dyes, lino, wood blocks etc.
- c. Two-Dimensional Art Equipment: Light table, spray gun, enlarger, computer etc.
- d. Improvisation of tools, materials and equipment;
- i. Two-Dimensional Art: colours, brushes, calligraphic pens etc.
- ii. Three-Dimensional Art: spatula, kiln, beater, etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- vi. maintain art tools;
- vii. use art materials;
- viii. operate and maintain art equipment;
- ix. improvise alternative tools, material and equipment;

## 

### 1. ART APPRECIATION

**Topics and notes**

- Man-made objects: architecture, sculpture etc: Natural phenomena: Zuma Rock, Ikogosi Warm Springs, etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- i. identify the aesthetic qualities of natural and man-made phenomena in Nigerian environment;
- ii. differentiate between natural and man-made aesthetic phenomena;

### 2. MEANING AND FUNCTIONS OF ART

**Topics and notes**

- IN SOCIETY
- a. Functions of art in the society: religious, social, cultural, political, therapeutic and economic needs.
- b. Functions of art in electronic and print media: advertisement, education, recreation etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- iii. examine the functions of art;
- iv. use art to enhance societal values;
- v. assess the role of art in electronic and print media;

### 3. ART ENTREPRENEURSHIP

**Topics and notes**

- Job prospects in the Visual Arts: Ceramist, Curator, Textile designer, Industrial designer, Sculptor, Photographer, Cartoonist, Illustrator etc.

**Candidates should be able to**

- vi. identify job opportunities in the Visual Arts.
