## Indices

**1. Simplify 2^5 × 2^-3 ÷ 2^2.**

- A. 1
- B. 2
- C. 4
- D. 8

**Key: A** — For the product add the powers: 5 + (−3) = 2. For the division subtract the power: 2 − 2 = 0. So the result is 2^0 = 1.

**2. Solve for x: 3^(x + 1) = 81.**

- A. 2
- B. 3
- C. 4
- D. 5

**Key: B** — Write 81 as a power of 3: 81 = 3^4. Then x + 1 = 4, so x = 3.

**3. Evaluate 27^(2/3).**

- A. 3
- B. 6
- C. 9
- D. 18

**Key: C** — 27 = 3^3, so 27^(2/3) = (3^3)^(2/3) = 3^2 = 9. The denominator of the fraction is the root; the numerator is the power.

**4. If 2^x = 1/8, what is x?**

- A. −4
- B. −3
- C. 3
- D. 4

**Key: B** — 1/8 = 1/2^3 = 2^−3. Since the bases are equal, x = −3.

**5. Simplify (x^3)^2 × x^−4, for x ≠ 0.**

- A. x
- B. x^2
- C. x^4
- D. x^6

**Key: B** — First multiply the powers inside the bracket: (x^3)^2 = x^6. Then add the exponents for the product: x^6 × x^−4 = x^(6 − 4) = x^2.

**6. Evaluate 16^(3/4).**

- A. 4
- B. 6
- C. 8
- D. 12

**Key: C** — 16^(1/4) = 2 because 2^4 = 16. Then raise that to the third power: 2^3 = 8.

**7. Solve 5^(2x − 1) = 125.**

- A. 1
- B. 3/2
- C. 2
- D. 5/2

**Key: C** — 125 = 5^3, so 2x − 1 = 3. Then 2x = 4 and x = 2.

**8. Simplify (8/27)^(−1/3).**

- A. 2/3
- B. 3/2
- C. 9/4
- D. 27/8

**Key: B** — A negative power flips the fraction: (8/27)^(−1/3) = (27/8)^(1/3). The cube root of 27 is 3 and of 8 is 2, so the answer is 3/2.

**9. If 9^x = 27, find x.**

- A. 1/2
- B. 3/2
- C. 2
- D. 3

**Key: B** — Write both sides to base 3: 9^x = (3^2)^x = 3^(2x) and 27 = 3^3. So 2x = 3 and x = 3/2.

**10. Simplify 4^(1/2) × 8^(1/3).**

- A. 2
- B. 4
- C. 6
- D. 8

**Key: B** — 4^(1/2) = 2 and 8^(1/3) = 2. Their product is 2 × 2 = 4.


## Surds

**1. Simplify √50.**

- A. 2√5
- B. 5√2
- C. 10√5
- D. 25√2

**Key: B** — Look for the largest square factor: 50 = 25 × 2. Then √50 = √25 × √2 = 5√2.

**2. Rationalise 1/√3.**

- A. √3/3
- B. 1/3
- C. √3
- D. 3√3

**Key: A** — Multiply numerator and denominator by √3: (1 × √3)/(√3 × √3) = √3/3.

**3. Simplify √18 + √8.**

- A. 5√2
- B. 6√2
- C. 2√13
- D. 13√2

**Key: A** — Simplify each term first: √18 = 3√2 and √8 = 2√2. Then 3√2 + 2√2 = 5√2.

**4. Simplify (√5 + 2)(√5 − 2).**

- A. 1
- B. 3
- C. 9
- D. 2√5

**Key: A** — This is the difference of two squares: (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b². So (√5)² − 2² = 5 − 4 = 1.

**5. Rationalise 2/(√7 − √3).**

- A. (√7 + √3)/2
- B. (√7 − √3)/2
- C. 2(√7 + √3)
- D. √7 + √3

**Key: A** — Multiply by the conjugate (√7 + √3)/(√7 + √3). The denominator becomes 7 − 3 = 4, so the expression is 2(√7 + √3)/4 = (√7 + √3)/2.

**6. Simplify √12 × √3.**

- A. 3√2
- B. 6
- C. 6√2
- D. 36

**Key: B** — Multiply under one root: √12 × √3 = √36 = 6.

**7. Which of the following is the largest?**

- A. √3
- B. √5
- C. 2
- D. √7

**Key: D** — Approximate: √3 ≈ 1.73, √5 ≈ 2.24, 2 = 2.00 and √7 ≈ 2.65. So √7 is the largest.

**8. Simplify √75 − √48.**

- A. √3
- B. 3√3
- C. 7√3
- D. 9√3

**Key: A** — √75 = 5√3 and √48 = 4√3. Subtracting gives 5√3 − 4√3 = √3.

**9. Simplify (√2 + 1)/(√2 − 1).**

- A. 1
- B. 3
- C. 3 + 2√2
- D. 3 − 2√2

**Key: C** — Multiply by (√2 + 1)/(√2 + 1). The denominator becomes 2 − 1 = 1, and the numerator becomes (√2 + 1)² = 2 + 2√2 + 1 = 3 + 2√2.

**10. If √x = 3√2, find x.**

- A. 6
- B. 9
- C. 12
- D. 18

**Key: D** — Square both sides: x = (3√2)² = 9 × 2 = 18.


## Quadratic equations

**1. Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.**

- A. x = 2 or x = 3
- B. x = −2 or x = −3
- C. x = 1 or x = 6
- D. x = −1 or x = −6

**Key: A** — Find two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to −5: −2 and −3. So (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0, giving x = 2 or x = 3.

**2. Solve x² + 7x + 12 = 0.**

- A. x = −3 or x = −4
- B. x = 3 or x = 4
- C. x = −2 or x = −6
- D. x = 2 or x = 6

**Key: A** — The numbers −3 and −4 multiply to 12 and add to 7. So (x + 3)(x + 4) = 0, giving x = −3 or x = −4.

**3. Solve 2x² − 5x + 2 = 0.**

- A. x = 1/2 or x = 2
- B. x = −1/2 or x = −2
- C. x = 1 or x = 4
- D. x = −1 or x = −4

**Key: A** — Factorise: 2x² − 5x + 2 = (2x − 1)(x − 2). Setting each factor to zero gives x = 1/2 or x = 2.

**4. Solve x² = 9.**

- A. x = 3 only
- B. x = −3 only
- C. x = 3 or x = −3
- D. x = 9 or x = −9

**Key: C** — Both 3² and (−3)² equal 9, so the equation has two solutions: x = 3 or x = −3.

**5. If x² − 4x + k = 0 has equal roots, find k.**

- A. −4
- B. 0
- C. 2
- D. 4

**Key: D** — Equal roots mean the discriminant b² − 4ac is zero. Here b = −4, a = 1 and c = k, so 16 − 4k = 0 and k = 4.

**6. Find the sum of the roots of 3x² − 9x + 6 = 0.**

- A. −3
- B. −1
- C. 1
- D. 3

**Key: D** — For ax² + bx + c = 0, the sum of the roots is −b/a. Here −(−9)/3 = 9/3 = 3.

**7. Find the product of the roots of 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0.**

- A. −3/2
- B. −5/2
- C. 3/2
- D. 5/2

**Key: A** — For ax² + bx + c = 0, the product of the roots is c/a. Here c = −3 and a = 2, so the product is −3/2.

**8. Solve (x − 3)(x + 2) = 0.**

- A. x = 3 or x = −2
- B. x = −3 or x = 2
- C. x = 3 or x = 2
- D. x = −3 or x = −2

**Key: A** — A product is zero when either factor is zero. x − 3 = 0 gives x = 3; x + 2 = 0 gives x = −2.

**9. Solve x² − 2x − 15 = 0.**

- A. x = 5 or x = −3
- B. x = −5 or x = 3
- C. x = 5 or x = 3
- D. x = −5 or x = −3

**Key: A** — The numbers 5 and −3 multiply to −15 and add to −2. So (x − 5)(x + 3) = 0, giving x = 5 or x = −3.

**10. If one root of x² − 7x + 10 = 0 is 2, find the other root.**

- A. 3
- B. 4
- C. 5
- D. 7

**Key: C** — Factorise: x² − 7x + 10 = (x − 2)(x − 5). The other root is x = 5. You can also use the sum of roots: 2 + r = 7, so r = 5.


## Trigonometry

**1. Find sin 30°.**

- A. 1/2
- B. √3/2
- C. 1/√3
- D. 1

**Key: A** — In a 30°–60°–90° triangle the side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse, so sin 30° = 1/2.

**2. Find cos 60°.**

- A. 1/2
- B. √3/2
- C. 1/√2
- D. 0

**Key: A** — cos 60° = 1/2. It is the same as sin 30° because 60° and 30° are complementary angles.

**3. Find tan 45°.**

- A. 0
- B. 1/2
- C. 1
- D. √3

**Key: C** — In a 45°–45°–90° triangle the two shorter sides are equal, so tan 45° = opposite/adjacent = 1.

**4. In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle θ is 3 cm and the hypotenuse is 5 cm. Find sin θ.**

- A. 3/5
- B. 4/5
- C. 3/4
- D. 5/3

**Key: A** — sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse. The opposite side is 3 and the hypotenuse is 5, so sin θ = 3/5.

**5. Which identity is true for every angle θ?**

- A. sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
- B. sin θ + cos θ = 1
- C. sin²θ − cos²θ = 1
- D. tan θ = sin θ cos θ

**Key: A** — For any point on the unit circle, the coordinates are (cos θ, sin θ) and the radius is 1. Pythagoras gives sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

**6. If sin θ = 3/5 and θ is acute, find cos θ.**

- A. 2/5
- B. 3/5
- C. 4/5
- D. 5/4

**Key: C** — Use sin²θ + cos²θ = 1: (3/5)² + cos²θ = 1, so cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25. Since θ is acute, cos θ = 4/5.

**7. Find tan 30°.**

- A. 1/2
- B. 1/√3
- C. √3/2
- D. √3

**Key: B** — tan 30° = sin 30°/cos 30° = (1/2)/(√3/2) = 1/√3, which is the same as √3/3.

**8. In triangle ABC, a = 7 cm, b = 5 cm and angle C = 60°. Find c².**

- A. 19
- B. 24
- C. 39
- D. 74

**Key: C** — By the cosine rule, c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C = 49 + 25 − 2(7)(5)(1/2) = 74 − 35 = 39.

**9. Find sin 45°.**

- A. 1/2
- B. √2/2
- C. √3/2
- D. 1

**Key: B** — In a 45°–45°–90° triangle the sides are in the ratio 1 : 1 : √2, so sin 45° = 1/√2 = √2/2.

**10. If cos θ = 0.6 and θ is acute, find sin θ.**

- A. 0.4
- B. 0.6
- C. 0.8
- D. 1.0

**Key: C** — sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ = 1 − 0.36 = 0.64. Since θ is acute, sin θ = √0.64 = 0.8.


## Probability

**1. A fair die is thrown once. What is the probability of getting an even number?**

- A. 1/6
- B. 1/3
- C. 1/2
- D. 2/3

**Key: C** — The even outcomes are 2, 4 and 6 — three out of six equally likely outcomes. So P(even) = 3/6 = 1/2.

**2. A fair coin is tossed twice. What is the probability of getting two heads?**

- A. 1/8
- B. 1/4
- C. 1/2
- D. 3/4

**Key: B** — The tosses are independent, so multiply: P(H) × P(H) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4.

**3. A bag contains 3 red and 2 blue balls. One ball is picked at random. What is the probability that it is red?**

- A. 2/5
- B. 1/2
- C. 3/5
- D. 3/2

**Key: C** — There are 3 red balls out of 5 balls in total, so P(red) = 3/5.

**4. If P(A) = 0.3, find P(not A).**

- A. 0.3
- B. 0.6
- C. 0.7
- D. 1.3

**Key: C** — An event and its complement cover all outcomes, so P(not A) = 1 − P(A) = 1 − 0.3 = 0.7.

**5. Two fair dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum is 7?**

- A. 1/12
- B. 1/9
- C. 1/6
- D. 7/36

**Key: C** — There are 36 equally likely outcomes. The pairs that sum to 7 are (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2) and (6,1) — six outcomes. So P(sum 7) = 6/36 = 1/6.

**6. A and B are mutually exclusive events with P(A) = 1/3 and P(B) = 1/4. Find P(A or B).**

- A. 1/12
- B. 1/7
- C. 7/12
- D. 3/4

**Key: C** — Mutually exclusive means they cannot happen together, so add: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.

**7. A and B are independent events with P(A) = 1/2 and P(B) = 1/3. Find P(A and B).**

- A. 1/6
- B. 1/3
- C. 1/2
- D. 5/6

**Key: A** — For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) = 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6.

**8. A card is drawn at random from a standard pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that it is an ace?**

- A. 1/52
- B. 1/26
- C. 1/13
- D. 1/4

**Key: C** — There are 4 aces in a pack of 52 cards, so P(ace) = 4/52 = 1/13.

**9. A bag contains 5 white and 3 black balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that both are white?**

- A. 5/14
- B. 25/64
- C. 5/8
- D. 3/8

**Key: A** — The first draw is white with probability 5/8. After removing one white ball, 4 white balls remain out of 7, so the second is white with probability 4/7. Multiply: 5/8 × 4/7 = 20/56 = 5/14.

**10. A fair coin is tossed twice. What is the probability of getting at least one head?**

- A. 1/4
- B. 1/2
- C. 2/3
- D. 3/4

**Key: D** — The only way to fail is to get two tails: P(TT) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4. So P(at least one head) = 1 − 1/4 = 3/4.
